Welcome to TrackRSmackR

The Cookie Bouncer is ready to work. Let's take 60 seconds to set up your safe list so you stay logged in everywhere you need to be — while we smack the trackers that follow you everywhere else.

How TrackRSmackR works: We intercept every cookie your browser receives, classify it by purpose, and sort it into one of two encrypted Cookie Jars. The Guest List Jar holds session-approved cookies — the ones you need to stay logged in. Encrypted, persistent, protected. Everything else gets bounced into the Bounced Jar: encrypted containment with a 30-second clock. When the timer hits zero, it's smashed. Gone. No recovery. All of this happens locally on your device. Nothing leaves your browser — except our TrackRSmackR Cookie Clones. Which don't clone your information. They are clones of our Gingerbread Men: a generic, non-identifiable, standardized decoy that replaces your real cookie data with cookie-cut data as a safety layer.
⚠️ About session cookies: Some cookies are actually necessary. Login sessions, shopping carts, and preferences all rely on cookies that your browser needs to keep you signed in. Big platforms like Google, Apple, and Microsoft use dozens of these across multiple domains. We'll help you decide which ones to trust — so you stay logged in without sacrificing your privacy everywhere else.
🍪 Meet the Gingerbread Men: For sites you don't allowlist, TrackRSmackR serves up a decoy — a synthetic cookie identity that's identical for every tracker, everywhere. They all get the same fake signal. No real you. No real profile. Just a Gingerbread Men.

Build Your Safe List

Check the platforms you use. TrackRSmackR will allow their session cookies so you stay logged in. Uncheck anything you want fully blocked — but read the warnings first.

🔵
Google Search, Gmail, YouTube, Drive, Maps, Chrome sync, and more
▼

⚠️ Google operates across many domains. Blocking these will sign you out of Gmail, YouTube, Google Drive, Maps, and any site that uses Sign in with Google. We recommend keeping these checked.

🍎
Apple iCloud, App Store, Apple ID, backups, iMessage web, and device services
▼

⚠️ Apple's services span many backend domains. Blocking these can break iCloud sync, Apple ID login, App Store purchases, iCloud backups, and Find My. If you use any Apple device or service, keep these checked.

🪟
Microsoft Outlook, OneDrive, Teams, Xbox, Office 365, Azure, and Windows services
▼

⚠️ Microsoft services rely on a shared authentication backend across dozens of domains. Blocking these can break Outlook, OneDrive, Teams, Office 365, Xbox, and Windows Update in browser.

💬
Social Platforms Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, Reddit, LinkedIn, TikTok
▼

⚠️ Social platforms are among the most aggressive trackers on the internet. Their session cookies are required to stay logged in — but they also embed trackers on millions of other sites. Unchecked by default. Check only the ones you actively use.

🛒
Shopping and Streaming Amazon, Netflix, Spotify, GitHub, Kickstarter, and more
▼

⚠️ Shopping and streaming sites require session cookies to maintain your cart, purchases, and playback state. Blocking these will sign you out and may lose cart contents.

Meet the Gingerbread Men

For sites not on your safe list, TrackRSmackR serves up a decoy — a synthetic cookie identity that's identical for every tracker, everywhere. They all get the same fake signal. No real you. No real profile.

How it works: Real trackers build profiles by collecting unique signals — your cookie IDs, your fingerprint, your behavior patterns. The Gingerbread Men scrambles that by presenting a standardized synthetic identity. Every tracker gets the same cookie. It's like giving every surveillance camera in the city the same face to photograph.
Same logic as Uniform Persona Mode: Just as we normalize your browser fingerprint so every site sees the same generic browser — we normalize your cookie identity so every tracker sees the same generic user. One Gingerbread Men. Millions of trackers. Zero profiles built on you.

🫚 Gingerbread Men Mode

Present a standardized synthetic cookie identity to all non-allowlisted sites. Trackers get a decoy. You get privacy.

👤 Uniform Persona Mode

Normalize your browser fingerprint so every site sees the same generic browser profile. No unique fingerprint to track.

🔄 Regenerative Cookie Shield

Detect and block cookies that attempt to respawn after deletion. Some trackers are persistent. We are more persistent.
